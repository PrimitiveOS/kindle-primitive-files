# Changelog

## 0.1.5

- The search field reverses black and white while tapped (it was light gray).

## 0.1.2

- Open supported books, documents, comics and images in Reader from the item menu.
- Offer CBR, CHM and XPS to an installed KOReader.

## 0.1.1

- Open PDFs, JPEGs, and PNGs in an installed Viewer. Use the item menu to open a supported document in KOReader.

## 0.1.0

- Browse and search files in Documents and other supported user folders.
- Create folders, rename entries, and copy or move files and folders. Delete files and empty folders after confirmation.
- Open supported books and PDFs in an installed KOReader from Launcher.
